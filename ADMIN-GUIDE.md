# Admin Guide — Managing Your Website

This guide is for school staff. It explains how to update the website yourself. No technical
knowledge needed. If anything looks different from this guide, contact your developer.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **`yourdomain.com/admin/login`** (or click **Staff Login** at the bottom of any page).
2. Enter the email and password you were given.
3. You'll land on the **Dashboard**, which shows how much content you have and any new
   contact enquiries.

Use the **sidebar** on the left to move between sections. Click **Sign out** at the bottom
when you're done (especially on shared computers).

> Anything you save appears on the public website within a few seconds — no need to "publish
> the site" or wait.

---

## The golden pattern (works for News, Events, Notices, Gallery, Faculty)

Every section works the same way:

1. Click the section in the sidebar → you see a **table** of existing items.
2. Click **Add** (top-right) to create, or the **pencil ✏️** to edit, or the **trash 🗑️** to delete.
3. Fill in the form and click **Save**. You're returned to the table and the change is live.

Use the **search box** above the table to find an item quickly.

---

## News

For announcements, achievements, and stories.

- **Title** — the headline.
- **Slug** — leave blank; it's created from the title automatically (it's the web address).
- **Category** — e.g. *Academics*, *Sports*, *Events* (shows as a small badge).
- **Excerpt** — one or two lines shown on the news listing.
- **Cover image** — upload a photo (or paste an image link). Landscape photos look best.
- **Body** — the full article. Use the toolbar for **bold**, *italic*, headings, and lists.
- **Publish date** — defaults to today.
- **Save as draft** — tick this to keep it hidden from the public until you're ready.

## Events

- **Event date** and **Venue** are shown on the events page and the home page.
- **Details** is the description (same editor as News).
- Events automatically move from **Upcoming** to **Past** once the date passes.

## Notices

For circulars and downloadable documents.

- **Title**, **Category** (general / examination / admission / circular / holiday), **Date**.
- **Document / PDF link** — paste a link to the PDF. Visitors click the notice to open it.

## Gallery

Photos are organised into **albums**.

- Give the album a **Title** and **Category**.
- Click **Add image** for each photo, upload it (or paste a link), and add a short
  **alt text** (a description — good for accessibility and search).
- On the public Gallery page, visitors can filter photos by category.

## Faculty

- **Name**, **Designation**, **Department**, **Qualifications**, optional **Photo** and **Bio**.
- **Display order** — lower numbers appear first. Staff are grouped by department on the site.

---

## Settings

Controls site-wide identity and contact details (used in the header, footer, and contact page):

- School name, tagline, logo
- Address, phone, email
- Social media links (leave blank to hide an icon)
- **Google Maps embed URL** for the contact page map

> To get the map URL: open Google Maps → search your school → **Share → Embed a map** →
> copy the `src="..."` link from the code it gives you, and paste that link here.

Click **Save settings** — the site updates immediately.

---

## Contact submissions (the inbox)

When someone uses the contact form, it appears here.

- Click **Resolve** once you've replied (or **Reopen** to undo).
- Click the **trash 🗑️** to delete spam.
- **Export CSV** downloads all enquiries as a spreadsheet.

---

## Tips

- **Images:** use clear, reasonably sized photos (under ~2 MB). Always add alt text.
- **Drafts:** use *Save as draft* to prepare content in advance.
- **Mistakes:** deleting can't be undone — but you can always re-create an item.

## Passwords & new accounts

There's no self-service password reset in the dashboard yet. To **reset a password** or **add
a new staff account**, contact your developer — it's a one-minute change on their end.
